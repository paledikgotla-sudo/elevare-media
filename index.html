Good questions.
Clear answers.

A few details to help you decide whether Elevare Media is the right creative partner for your next project.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We review your brief and reply by email to clarify your goals, deliverables, timing and budget. If the project is a good fit, we prepare a clear scope and quotation before any work begins.

Do you work with businesses outside Gaborone?

Yes. Many design, content-planning and editing projects can be handled remotely. Any on-location work must be discussed and agreed in advance.

Can I request just one design or video?

Yes. You can enquire about a one-off flyer, poster, campaign graphic, brand asset or short-form video. The final price depends on the brief and complexity.

Do your packages include advertising spend?

No. Paid advertising budgets, printing, third-party subscriptions and other external costs are separate unless specifically included in a written quotation.

Do you guarantee sales, followers or viral results?

No agency can responsibly guarantee those outcomes. We focus on clear creative, consistent execution and agreed deliverables. Where tracking is available, we can review performance and recommend improvements.

How do payments and project terms work?

Before starting, we confirm the deliverables, timeline, price, revision scope and payment schedule in writing. Any external costs are agreed separately.
